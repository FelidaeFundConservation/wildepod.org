Deleting Your Wilde Backyard Account

Last updated 7/25/2024

If you no longer wish to use Wilde Backyard, you can delete your account by navigate to the Profile Page. Then, press the Delete Account button, and follow the instructions.

Account deletion is irreversible and effective immediately, so please be certain you want to remove your account. Also note that your posts and comments will remain in our database as per the Terms of Service.
