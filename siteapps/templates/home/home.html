Welcome to WildePod

Let's make our community a better place! Help us record trail camera data.

WildePod Database

We have built a database repository for wildlife imagery collected on public, state, county, city and private lands. Our work is focused on conservation of wild fields but we collect images of all wildlife for use in research and land planning. Clean Data.

WildePod Backyard

We are building a community backyard program that partners with residential communities to collect wildlife images on private properties. If you think you have wildlife moving through your property or have seen it, we want to work with you!

DNA Research

We collect scat, hair and eDNA for our research on mountain lions and bobcats in the SF Bay Area in order to determine impacts of human activities on the health of felid species at the urban edge. Contact us for more information or to get involved.
